# Visual pass

Implements the eight visual items requested from the outer `FutureWork.docx` and `HANDOFF.md`.

## Changes

1. Cliff and mesa extrusion, with authored rock faces and cliff caps. Collision and surface-height rules were not changed.
2. Ashfall includes a 4.2-second, viewport-sized dragon flyover with an animated fire plume. Existing damage and story outcomes remain in their original steps.
3. Roofs cover the raised north wall. The family facade stays 2.08 tiles deep. Only the entered house's front wall is cut away; far and side walls remain. Fountain columns and tiered spouts are depth-sorted upright sprites.
4. Regional shrine exteriors are open-air marble courts with low boundaries, guardian statues, an altar, offerings and candles. Their original footprint, threshold and puzzle remain. Activation refreshes cached prop classifications.
5. Surviving Elderbrook inn/store/herb keepers receive bandages, soot and torn-hem dressing. Fallen/dead villagers and other villages do not. The low-HP glow and flash are narrowed without changing the HP threshold.
6. Glide has a rise, travel and landing arc with an airborne pose and ground shadow. This is presentation only: logical arrival and its hooks keep their existing timing. The transient arc clears on camera snaps, load, map changes and death.
7. Air and Lightning overworlds derive up to three decorative spillways from existing cloud/storm rims. They fall into the void and fade into spray. No tiles, cave links, entrances or generation recipes are added.
8. The outer lanes of wide waterfalls use the regional standing-water sheet. The centre, single-row crest, single-row foot and hidden doorway retain falling-water art. Wrapped streaks are clipped to their tiles.

New art is generated by the existing Aseprite Lua generators. The original 22 village frames and 36 dragon frames were pixel-compared against the pre-pass sheets and remain unchanged. There are no new runtime dependencies or script tags.

## Scope and concurrent work

This pass did not implement armor scaling, shrine rewards, cave generation, chest rewards, shop contents or the water-freeze fix.

During implementation, the separate mechanics pass committed `84bc191`. It included the visual Glide hooks already edited in shared `abilities.js` and `player.js`. Those hooks are therefore already committed, while the remaining visual changes are a working-tree diff. No commit or push was performed by this visual pass. The mechanics commit was preserved, not reverted.
